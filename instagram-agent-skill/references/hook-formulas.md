# 21 hook formulas (Reels: first ~2 seconds spoken + on-screen text)
1. Contrarian: "Stop doing X. Do Y."
2. Specific result: "I got [number] [outcome] in [time]."
3. Mistake: "The [N] mistakes making your [thing] fail."
4. Withheld: "Nobody tells you this about [thing]."
5. Before/after: "This was [bad state]. Now [good state]."
6. Question gap: "Why does [surprising thing] happen?"
7. Direct callout: "If you [specific situation], watch this."
8. Myth bust: "[Common belief] is wrong."
9. Time-boxed: "Do this in [N] seconds."
10. Ranking: "The only [N] [things] worth [verb]."
11. Story open: "Last [time], [specific event] happened."
12. Confession: "I wasted [time/money] on [thing]."
13. Cost: "This is costing you [thing]."
14. Tutorial promise: "How to [outcome] without [pain]."
15. Reaction/stitch: "Look at what [thing] just did."
16. Comparison: "[A] vs [B]: one wins."
17. Warning: "Don't [verb] until you see this."
18. Curiosity object: show odd object, hold 1s, then name it.
19. Pattern interrupt: unexpected visual/sound, then claim.
20. Social proof: "[N] people asked me about [thing]."
21. Challenge: "Try this for [N] days."

Rules: payoff promised in line one; no "hey guys" / "so today" / "in this video"; one idea per hook.
